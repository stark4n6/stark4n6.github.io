A short and sweet post to announce that the DFIR Discord community book The Hitchhiker's Guide to DFIR: Experiences From Beginners and Experts has officially reached release v1.0. Kudos to all the authors and contributors so far. I had the pleasure of writing a chapter on Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges and tips. If you want to contribute and write a chapter, reach out in the Discord channel (here).
